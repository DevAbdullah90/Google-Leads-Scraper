# Email Template Reference

## Subject Line Examples

### Based on Rating
- "Help [Business] capture every WhatsApp lead 24/7"
- "[Business] - Your 4.9 rating deserves 24/7 WhatsApp support"

### Based on Reviews
- "With [X] reviews, [Business] needs instant WhatsApp responses"
- "[Business] - Never miss a lead from your [X] monthly inquiries"

### Based on Location
- "[Business] - Automate WhatsApp for your Abu Dhabi properties"
- "24/7 WhatsApp support for [Business]'s Abu Dhabi clients"

### Based on Category
- "[Business] - AI assistant for your real estate inquiries"
- "[Business] - Automate property inquiry responses"

## Email Body Templates

### Template 1: Rating + Reviews Focus

```
Dear [Business Name] Team,

I came across your Google listing and was impressed by your [Rating] rating with [Reviews] reviews - that's a clear sign clients trust your expertise in Abu Dhabi real estate.

However, I noticed a common challenge many agencies face: WhatsApp inquiries that come in after hours or during busy periods often get delayed responses. In real estate, speed matters - the first agency to respond usually wins the client.

We've built an AI-powered WhatsApp assistant specifically for real estate agencies like yours. It instantly replies to every property inquiry 24/7, answers questions about listings, shares property details, and qualifies leads automatically. When a client is ready for a viewing, your sales team gets notified instantly.

The result? Faster response times, more qualified leads, and no missed opportunities - all without hiring additional staff.

Would you be open to a 15-minute call to see how this could work for [Business Name]?

Best regards,
[SIGNATURE]
```


## Personalization Elements

### From Business Name
- Use exact business name from spreadsheet
- Example: "Almira Real Estate" not "Almira"

### From Rating
- Reference specific rating: "4.8 rating"
- Use descriptive words: "impressive", "excellent", "outstanding"

### From Reviews
- Reference review count: "380 reviews"
- Imply trust: "clear sign clients trust your expertise"

### From Location
- Reference specific area: "Abu Dhabi", "Al Reem Island", "Electra Street"
- Show local knowledge

### From Category
- Real estate agent: Focus on personal service
- Real estate agency: Focus on team coverage
- Real estate consultant: Focus on expertise

## What NOT to Say

- "AI-powered" (use "smart assistant" or "automated")
- "LLM" or "large language model"
- "API integration"
- "Machine learning"
- "Chatbot" (use "assistant" or "helper")
- "Automation workflow"
- "Technical implementation"

## What TO Say

- "Instantly replies"
- "24/7 support"
- "Never miss a lead"
- "Faster response times"
- "More qualified leads"
- "Better customer experience"
- "Without hiring additional staff"
- "Automatically handles"
- "Smart assistant"
